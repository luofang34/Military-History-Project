# Chastise dam photographs (review)

Three photographs of the Möhne and Eder dams after the raid, each shown from its solved
camera with the photograph draped on the present-day surface. The package is for reviewing
the poses before the photographs join `missions/operation-chastise`.

Open it with
<https://sokoly.app/?operation=https://history.sokoly.app/reviews/dam-photographs&recorded-path>.

Each pose was fitted by hand-matched structure (dam crest and towers) on the reference
surface, then refined against the crest edges in the photograph; automatic image matching
does not confirm any of them. Each replay's `registration` gives the camera, the method and
the residual.
